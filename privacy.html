Privacy Policy

Leon's Ledger · Effective 26 November 2026

The short version
  • Your budget lives on your device. Nothing is uploaded unless you create an account to sync or share, or use the AI features.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics.
  • AI questions and receipt photos are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to get an answer. We don't store them.
  • You can export your data any time, and delete your account from Settings, which removes your data from our server.

Who we are

Leon's Ledger ("the app", "we") is a personal budgeting app made by Leon Markarian, Lebanon. Contact: leonmarkarian99@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter — incomes, payments, buckets, your split plan, wallets, goals, notes, AI chat history, your PIN and any personal AI key — is stored on your phone or computer. Your PIN, personal API key and AI chat history never leave your device, even if you use an account.

AI features

When you ask the AI advisor a question, request a report or scan a receipt, the app sends your question (or photo), a summary of the current budget (balances, plan, goals, recent activity), the “About me” text and your last few chat messages to Anthropic, which provides the AI model, through our server. Our server forwards the request and returns the answer; it does not save your questions, photos or answers. Anthropic processes this data under its own privacy policy and commercial terms. AI answers are general guidance and can be wrong.

Shared budgets

If you create or join a shared budget, everything in that budget — including entries you add and your username — is visible to all of its members. Your personal budget is never shared. Anyone with a valid invite code can join, so share codes only with people you trust; codes expire after 7 days.

Service providers

  • Cloudflare — runs our server and stores account and synced data. Like any web service it processes IP addresses to deliver requests and protect against abuse.
  • Anthropic — provides the AI model for the advisor, reports and receipt scanning.
  • GitHub — hosts the website, the phone version and the app's update downloads.

How long we keep data

Account and synced data are kept until you delete them or your account. Daily usage counters are only needed for the current day. If you leave a shared budget, it stays with its other members; when the last member leaves, it is deleted.

Your choices and rights

  • Use it without an account — everything stays on your device.
  • Export your data any time (Settings → Backup, or Reports → Excel/PDF).
  • Correct or delete any entry in the app.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Account security. This removes your account and your synced budgets from our server (shared budgets remain for their other members).
  • Questions or requests: leonmarkarian99@gmail.com.

Security

Connections use HTTPS, passwords and recovery codes are hashed, and an optional PIN locks the app on your device. Synced data is protected on our server but is not end-to-end encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your recovery code private.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country).

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date, and mention important changes in the app.
